Build a legal moveset

A Pokémon can learn moves from its own types plus the Normal pool. The planner checks that rule, keeps the starting move, and limits the draft to four distinct moves; it does not estimate a fictional DPS score.

Choose a starter build path

Two source-checked upgrade paths to learn with. These are planning examples, not tested best builds or guaranteed clears.

fire

Charmander: Ember → Fire Blast

Keep Ember in slot 1. Add Fire Spin only when you want a different attack pattern.

  1. 1Start with Ember

    Use Ember while moving through open ground. It fires toward a nearby enemy.

  2. 2Raise it to level 5

    Choose its move-upgrade cards when offered. Take Power during this run when it appears; either condition can be completed first.

  3. 3Select Fire Blast

    Once both conditions are met, choose the evolution card when offered at a level-up. It does not happen automatically.

OPTIONAL SWITCHING ATTACK

Fire Spin

Try Fire Spin when enemies close in from several sides. Switch back to Ember when you want a directed projectile; do not move into a crowd just to use the orbit.

Inspect Fire Spin →

Diagrams show attack patterns, not exact range or damage. Before bonds, only the active attack slot fires; adding Fire Spin does not make both attacks fire together. Use 1–4, Q/E or a move-slot tap to switch.

If the run does not follow the plan

No Power card yet?

Continue upgrading Ember when available. If neither is offered, choose an upgrade that helps your immediate situation, such as Vigor for health or Sprint for movement. They do not replace Power in this recipe.

No Fire Spin offered?

If Fire Spin is not offered, keep improving Ember. A second attack is optional, so leave room instead of filling every slot.

Both conditions met, but no evolution?

Check that you upgraded this move, not just your partner, and took Power in this run. Menu upgrades do not count. An eligible move still needs its evolution card to be selected.

Weather fades in and out over 3 seconds; this tool uses its full-strength value. Terrain damage, weather chip damage, partner stats, critical hits and defensive bonuses are separate. Both player and enemy attacks use type/weather modifiers.

Is this the best build?

This is an example of a legal upgrade path, not a tested ranking. Enemy positioning, upgrade offers and permanent stats affect each run.

Can I use Water moves on Charmander?

Not through the ordinary pool in this version. Its pool contains Fire and Normal moves; use the character profile to see the complete list.

Rules: fixed game revision b297089. moves.js · levelup.js

01

Begin with the starting move

Each partner receives a starting move based on its types, unless a specific override exists. Keep that move in your plan. Plan its evolution first, then choose additional attacks that address a different range or positioning problem.

02

Use the legal pool

A Grass/Poison partner has Grass, Poison and Normal moves. A Fire partner does not gain Water attacks simply because a main-series Pokémon game allows a similar move. This site imports the actual poolFor rule from PokéSurvivor.

03

Plan for switching

A projectile and a close-range move offer different positioning choices. Switch deliberately instead of expecting all slots to deal damage simultaneously. The game prevents offering a third buff; the planner also rejects drafts with more than two.

04

Treat bonds separately

Defeating a legendary can enable a bond that combines moves in a slot. This first planner models unbonded slots only. It does not claim a particular bond order or simultaneous-attack benefit without modeling those rules.

05

Make a useful draft

Choose a goal, a few legal moves and their required run cards. Save the draft with a clear name. After real runs, record the permanent upgrades, device, outcomes and failures before calling a build tested.

Common mistakes & limits

  • A legal build may still be awkward to control.
  • Damage and cooldown alone do not describe area, hits or real performance.
  • This site has not assigned tested rankings to the starter drafts.
