Pikachu First Run Build

A legal Pikachu starter draft with evolution requirements, alternatives and clearly stated testing limits.

Source checked · b29708914 Oct 2026
Source-checked draft · Not playtested · No survival-time or “best build” claim

First-run goal: learn Thunder Shock

Kanto regional gacha · rarity 2★. No permanent-upgrade level is assumed; your actual upgrades change the outcome.

Moves and priorities

  1. Begin with Thunder Shock. Practice its projectile behavior while keeping space to move.
  2. Raise this move toward level 5. Take Power in the same run to qualify for Thunder.
  3. Try Discharge as a second legal option; switch active slots deliberately.
  4. Leave the remaining slots open until you understand what your run needs. More filled slots do not mean more simultaneous attacks.

Alternatives and tradeoffs

Tackle, Quick Attack, Swords Dance, Agility are also legal options. Pick an alternative for its behavior and range rather than assuming a larger damage number wins every encounter.

Failure risks

Spreading levels across too many moves can delay the first evolution. A close-range option demands different positioning from a projectile. This draft does not model bonds, weather, items or a complete permanent-upgrade profile.

Evidence and testing task

Legality comes from revision b297089. No recorded runs have been used to label this build Tested. Before promoting it, record at least three runs with device, permanent upgrades, cards, survival time and boss outcomes; disclose the small sample.
