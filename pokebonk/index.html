YOUR FIRST FOUR MINUTES

Know what to do next

Build your team

Choose a partner, create a room and share its six-character code. Friends choose Join room; the host starts. Move with WASD or arrow keys; attacks fire automatically.

Build a useful combo

Collect blue XP crystals. Learn up to three moves and upgrade each to rank 3.

Ember + Spark: +15% damage.
Bubble + Pulse: 20% shorter attack intervals.

Keep the team fighting

Stay near a fallen teammate for three seconds to revive them. Use the on-screen Q / E actions on touch devices and the touch movement pad to move.

Open My progress for collection and graphics options.

Before you start

How do friends join?

Create a room and share the code. Each player needs access to the same game server and a separate browser session. Share this page with your friends, then send the room code. Up to four players can join. If the server is full, try again later.

Will my progress survive a refresh?

Guest collection is saved on this game server and accessed through this browser’s guest credentials. Keep site data: clearing browser data loses access, and there is no email recovery. A short disconnect keeps your role for 60 seconds; server recovery may resume from the latest checkpoint. Longer interruptions may end the room.

Can I pause, or make the game easier to see?

Use the pause icon (or P) to pause the room. Only the host can pause and resume; all teammates see the same paused state. Opening a panel alone does not pause battle. In My progress, lower graphics quality or effect opacity; sound starts muted. Leave danger before reading a panel.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE OPENING

What is PokeBonk?

PokeBonk is Charcher’s free, unofficial browser fan game, inspired by Megabonk. You move a partner through a 3D arena while attacks fire automatically. Level-up choices shape the run. The original project says it is played on its website, with no download distribution.

FROM THE ENTRY SCREEN TO A RUN

Your first four steps

Use the English interface to match these screenshots. All four steps stay visible; no checklist is required to play.

01

Enter as a guest

Open pokebonk.com, choose Guest, then Play as a guest! No account creation is needed for this entry. Returning players may already see the menu.

02

Choose your first partner

Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle, then confirm the named button below the cards. The screen says the other two require their challenges and capture later; they are not all immediately in your League.

03

Confirm a League member

From the main menu, Play opens The League. Read your available partner’s kit, then press I choose you! or Enter. Our walkthrough uses Squirtle.

04

Prepare and start

Choose an available region and mode. Classic offers difficulty choices; the Survival preparation panel we checked did not. We played Survival in Viridian Forest. Press Start! and let the scene load.

A small first goal

Move comfortably, pause to inspect your kit, then read one upgrade. Our Water Gun attacked while we moved; selecting Bubble at level 2 added a second damaging move. This short check is not a completed run.

Automatic or manual aim?

Automatic targets the closest enemy. Manual points toward the camera. Q toggles the mode. The pause help also lists P as a way to pause in fullscreen without Esc taking you out of fullscreen.

See the current control screens and testing limits

Movement, automatic attacks and Esc pause were used in our short desktop run. Other bindings are transcribed from the game’s settings and help; we have not tested every action, a physical controller or mobile touch play.

Why are these evolution levels different from the Pokémon games?

PokeBonk converts the base evolution data into run-level thresholds, and some partners have their own overrides. The three starters above use the standard conversion: the first stage is level 24; the final stage is 41 for Venusaur and 44 for Charizard and Blastoise. Do not apply these levels to every partner or use our PokeSurvivor move-evolution table for this game.

These are code-reviewed thresholds, not completed evolution playtests. The current in-game version and partner details take priority if an update changes them.

WHEN THE CARD YOU WANT DOES NOT APPEAR

Find out which moves can appear next

Pause and open Still to get in the Moves panel. This is the game’s reference for moves that can still appear as you level up.

I reached the level. Where is my kit move?

A level requirement makes the move eligible for an offer; it does not automatically teach it. The game builds randomized upgrade offers and excludes moves you already have. Check your available slots as well.

Can I get moves outside my partner’s four-move kit?

Yes. The rules also consider supported moves from the species’ learning line, including earlier forms, and shared kit moves with their own type and League conditions. The full Pokémon move database is not your current offer pool.

Does the original move power equal my damage here?

No. PokeBonk translates moves into attack patterns with its own damage, cooldown, projectiles and area. Base Pokémon power alone cannot tell you which card is better. Read the actual card and inspect damage in pause.

What did the playtest actually confirm?

Squirtle started with Water Gun. At run level 2, Bubble appeared as an offer; after choosing it, both moves recorded damage in pause. The other starter paths and later evolution thresholds above were checked in the published rules, not played through.

Choose the effect your current kit can use

Our PP Up offer named Water Gun under “Affects”; Stardust warned that none of our moves used its area effect. Check those lines before rarity or a familiar move name.

BEFORE YOU SEARCH AGAIN

Quick answers and a next step

Is PokeBonk free, and do I need a download?

The original project describes it as free and non-profit, played on pokebonk.com. Its project notice says it does not distribute a download. Our PokeBonk icon opens that website.

Is PokeBonk an official Pokémon game?

No. It is a fan project led by Charcher, independent of Nintendo and the Pokémon rights holders. This guide is also independent of the game project.

Can I begin without registering?

Yes. We used Guest → Play as a guest! and reached a real run. The menu says guests do not appear on the leaderboard. Account features and save recovery were not tested.

Why are my menus or cards different?

Check the version beneath the original game logo and your language setting. These screenshots are v0.6.2 in English. Your unlocked partners, mode and random upgrade offers can differ.

The camera or attacks feel wrong. What should I check?

Open Settings → Controls and check Automatic versus Manual aiming. Open Settings → Keys for your actual bindings. Start with one change and compare what it does.

What if the game is blank, slow or my progress seems missing?

Confirm that you are on pokebonk.com. For performance, inspect its Graphics settings. For missing progress, preserve the current browser session and record the version and symptom before contacting the original project. Do not start by clearing site data. This guide cannot inspect or restore its private saves.

Can I use a phone or play with friends?

This guide verifies a desktop Guest opening only. It does not establish current mobile compatibility or released co-op. Check the original game and its current announcements; a clan feature alone does not prove multiplayer play.
