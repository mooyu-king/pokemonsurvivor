BEFORE YOU SET OUT

A few useful answers

Is this another Pokémon game?

No. Aetheria has its own fantasy world, three adventurer classes and a three-region campaign. It does not use your PokéSurvivor team or progress.

Do attacks happen automatically?

Basic auto-attack starts enabled. Position your hero, use Q for your class skill and Shift to dodge. You can turn auto-attack off in the game.

How do I keep my progress?

The game saves in this browser. Use Pause → Export save before switching browsers or websites; import the JSON file where you want to continue.

Can I play with friends?

The game embedded here is single-player. The original Aetheria Online is a separate MMORPG; its guilds, PvP and group dungeons are not included in this edition.
