A SURVIVAL GAME WITH A FAMILIAR FACE

What is Pokemon Survivor?

PokéSurvivor is JPTER78’s browser survival game: choose a Pokémon, dodge growing enemy crowds and collect experience to shape your attacks. Your active move fires automatically, leaving you to decide where to move and when to switch.

This independent wiki pairs a local solo edition with source-checked guides and planning tools. It covers the browser game, not Pokémon Survivor: Burst or other similarly named projects.

Source checked · b29708914 Oct 2026

Before you jump in

Do I need an account?

No. This edition uses local solo play. Progress stays in this browser when storage is available; cloud accounts and online co-op are disabled.

Can I play on mobile?

The game has touch controls. Landscape is recommended; fullscreen uses a page immersion fallback when the browser cannot enter native fullscreen. Real-device compatibility still requires acceptance testing.

How do I unlock more Pokémon?

Choose a regional gacha banner and spend tickets earned in play. Each banner has separate pity. Pikachu and Eevee are collection unlocks in this version, not initial partner choices.

Is this an official Pokémon website?

No. This is an independent fan site. The original game is by JPTER78, and Pokémon belongs to its respective rights holders.
